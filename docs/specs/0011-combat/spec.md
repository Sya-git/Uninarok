## 1. Why

After SPEC-0010 the player walks among monsters but cannot touch them: a click on a poring walks onto its cell, a monster that bites shows nothing, the character never dies, and loot cannot be picked up. Combat is the core loop of the game and the first place where client timing is visible to the player. The server sends one packet per blow with the attacker's and the target's motion times; if the client ignores them, attacks look cropped, monsters flinch late or never, and the attack request is refused because the client thinks it is in range when the server does not. Skills (0012) and effects (0013) reuse everything here: the damage display, the hit timing, the HP bars and the target rules.

## 2. Goals

1. The player attacks a monster with a click and keeps attacking it, walking into range as the server requires, until it dies.
2. Every blow in view shows as the original client shows it: attack motion, projectile of ranged attacks, hurt motion, damage number, miss, critical, multi-hit, at the server's timing.
3. The own character's HP, SP, levels and EXP are always the server's; it dies, and comes back at its save point.
4. Monsters show HP bars, the own character its HP and SP bars, and loot can be picked up.
5. Everything above is testable without a server, and a dev HUD shows what the client believes.

## 3. Non-goals

- Skills, cast bars, skill damage packets, ground skill units (SPEC-0012); hit, critical and level-up effects, STR, sounds (SPEC-0013).
- PvP and GvG targeting (clicking a player or an NPC never attacks); homunculus, mercenary and pet commands.
- Chat, the basic info window, the inventory window and any inventory model beyond the result of a pickup, item use, equipment, party HP bars, GM HP meters, the "return to character select" option.
- Client-side prediction of attacks or damage, and lag compensation: the client shows what the server sends, when it arrives (as SPEC-0010).

## 5. Success criteria

- From Bootstrap, on a local Hercules: walk to prt_fild08, kill 20 monsters with continuous attack, pick up their loot, then die and return to the save point. No attack is refused for distance more than once per target, the own HP, SP and EXP equal the server's at the end (HUD versus `@stats` and `@exp`), every monster's last bar matches its HP, and no unit is left behind.
- The FR-6, FR-7 and FR-14 side-by-side evidence with the original client is saved.
- FR-12: 0 B per frame from the combat update under its load.

## 6. Assumptions and constraints

- Hercules is the reference (`src/map/clif.cpp`, `unit.cpp`, `battle.cpp`, `pc.cpp`, `path.cpp`, `conf/map/battle/*.conf`). Facts read on 2026-11-21 that decide behaviour:
  - **The server does not walk a player into range.** `unit_attack_timer_sub` checks the client distance and, if too far, sends `ZC_ATTACK_FAILURE_FOR_DISTANCE` and stops; only monsters chase on the server. A request received while the player walks is remembered and run at the next full step if the target is then in range (`stepaction`). So approaching is the client's job (FR-3).
  - The range check uses the server's cell of each unit, which lags the shown position by up to one step (SPEC-0010 plan §9); FR-3 measures from the own server cell for that reason.
  - `attackMT` is the attacker's `amotion`, `attackedMT` the target's `dmotion` sent raw (`WALKDELAY_SYNC` is not defined in `src/config/core.h`); the damage is applied `amotion` after the blow (`delay_battle_damage: true`).
  - Damage types (`enum battle_dmg_type`): 0 normal, 4 endure, 8 multi-hit, 9 endured multi-hit, 10 critical, 11 lucky dodge; a hit with no target motion and damage > 0 is turned into endure (`clif_calc_delay`). The SP damage flag is always 0.
  - From PACKETVER 20131223 a monster's HP reaches the client by re-sending its walking-unit entry (`0x09FD`, `HP`/`maxHP` fields, −1 when hidden) after each damage; `UNIT_HP` `0x0977` is only sent by older versions (`mob.cpp`, `clif.cpp` under `#if PACKETVER < 20131223`). Its packet doc is corrected in the plan.
  - Own death is `UNIT_VANISH` reason 1 for the own id; respawn (`CZ_RESTART` type 0) is `pc_setpos` to the save point, which sends a map change even on the same map; a revive in place is `ZC_RESURRECTION`.
  - Pickup: server distance ≤ 2 cells (square), every failure answers with `ZC_ITEM_PICKUP_ACK` and a result other than 0: 6 for a refusal before the item is given (too far, reserved loot, dead, cannot act), the inventory's own code when it cannot take the item (2 overweight, 4 no free slot, 5 maximum amount, 7 stack limit); the pickup motion is `ZC_NOTIFY_ACT` `0x008A` with action 1 to the area.
- Packet IDs and layouts depend on PACKETVER 20220406 and are each checked against `packets_struct.h`, `clif.cpp` and a real capture in the plan, with their docs in the same change (BACKLOG). Expected: in `0x08C8` damage, `0x008A` act (pickup motion), `0x0139` too far, `0x013A` attack range, `0x00B0`, `0x00B1` and `0x0ACB` status values (EXP as 64-bit), `0x0ACC` EXP gain, `0x0148` resurrection, `0x09FD` monster HP, `0x0B41` pickup result, `0x0080` and `0x00A1` (SPEC-0010); out `0x0437` attack (actions 0 and 7), `0x0118` stop attack, `0x0362` pickup, `0x00B2` restart.
- Server values (`attack_walk_delay`, `show_monster_hp_bar`, `display_fake_hp_when_dead`, attack range) come from the server; the client follows what it receives and keeps Hercules's defaults only where it must assume one.
- Display of blows uses SPEC-0009's play-once and hold animations; timing uses SPEC-0010's server clock only where a packet carries a server time; blows start when they arrive (no lag compensation).
- Projectiles (FR-14): the damage packet says nothing about weapons or projectiles, so the client decides from what it knows of the attacker: a player's weapon view id, a monster's job id. The original vthibault/roBrowser draws no basic-attack projectile. roBrowserLegacy (MrAntares; read 2026-11-21, `Engine/MapEngine/Entity.js`, `Renderer/Effects/ArrowFlight.js`, `DB/Monsters/AttackEffectTable.js`) does the following:
  - **Players:** an arrow for bow types only (bow, crossbow, arbalest, kakkung, hunter bow, bow of Rudra). Guns, throwing weapons, whips and instruments get none.
  - **Monsters:** an arrow for a hard-coded table of job ids (archer skeleton, orc, goblin, raydric and kobold archers, archer guardian, gargoyle, archer mercenaries). Other monsters in that table get their own projectiles (parasite, stone shooter, poton cannon and others).
  - **Player timing:** the arrow is released at the attack's hit frame and flies 8 frames of 24 ms (192 ms) at 8 cells or more, less when closer. The damage number waits for it.
  - **Monster timing:** the arrow starts at `attackMT` and the number does not wait.

  Its header claims these timings match the official client (kRO, 2025). None of this is confirmed yet. The plan checks every case against the original client, the projectile sprite included, and records the result. A monster projectile other than the arrow that needs an effect from SPEC-0013 is drawn there; this spec gives it the launch and hit moments.
- Attack animation (FR-15): Hercules sends the right hand as the weapon view and the left hand as the shield view, each the item's view id if it has one, else its item id (`clif_get_weapon_view`); katars (item ids 1250 to 1260) have view 16. roBrowserLegacy (read 2026-11-23, `DB/Jobs/WeaponAction.js`, `DB/Items/EquipmentRules.js`) copies a first-class row to each derived job and combines both hands into one weapon type for animation and sprite; the left hand alone is treated as the only weapon. In the editor, 129 job ids of its table differed from the client's before this revision; ids above 4242 (second outfits, Hyper Novice, Spirit Handler, Soul Ascetic, Night Watch, Druid family) were inferred from the table, not run. Taekwon and Star Gladiator have no row in roBrowserLegacy; the owner observed them playing `Attack2` for every weapon in the original client (2026-11-23).
- The original client is the reference for what the player sees (numbers, bars, prompt, cursor); where roBrowser is used as a lead, the original client confirms it.
- Dev-only code is stripped from release builds (project rule).
- Game UI (the restart prompt of FR-8, and the UI of later specs) is built with UI Toolkit, not with Unity's built-in UI (uGUI) or IMGUI. Dev-only views may stay as SPEC-0010 builds them.
