# T0.4: the original client's Esc prompt

- **File:** `restart-prompt-original.png` (a cropped screenshot, about 345 x 115 px).
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-23, the original Ragnarok Online client.

## What it shows

- One small window over the game map (yellow-orange ground and a bit of green at the left edge).
- Title bar: a thin blue-to-white gradient with a small round icon at the left and the text **"Game setting window (251-437)"** in black. The title bar is about 17 px high.
- Body: white, with a thin dark-blue/grey border; about 280 px wide and 90 px high in the crop.
- Two full-width buttons, stacked, in this order from top to bottom:
  1. **"Go to saved area"**
  2. **"Return to game"**
- Buttons: about 215 px wide and 20 px high, light-grey vertical gradient, rounded corners, thin grey border, black centred text of about 12 px, a few pixels of gap between them and equal side margins.

## What it does not show

- What Esc does while the window is open, and what its close behaviour is (no close box is visible in the title bar).
- Whether this is also the window shown on death, or only the Esc window of a living character.
- What "(251-437)" means. Probably a message id or debug suffix of the original (unconfirmed); the client does not reproduce it.
- Where the window sits on the screen (the crop has no screen frame), and its hover or pressed look.
- The `esc_*.bmp` images (no extracted sprites were delivered).
